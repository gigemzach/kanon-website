Estate planning is a legal exercise. Estate *tax* planning is an accounting one, and the two need to agree with each other.

We work on the tax side, generally alongside an estate attorney and often alongside a financial adviser.

## Scope

- Lifetime gift planning, annual exclusion mechanics, and Form 709 preparation
- Portability elections and the return that has to be filed to preserve them
- Basis planning — the trade-off between removing assets from the estate and preserving a step-up
- Fiduciary income tax returns for estates and trusts
- Income in respect of a decedent and the associated deduction
- Coordination with the attorney's documents so the tax filings match the structure that was actually created

## What this is not

This is tax planning and compliance. It is not legal drafting, it is not investment advice, and it is not asset management. We work with your attorney rather than replacing them, and we are not a registered investment adviser.
