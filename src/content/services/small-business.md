There is a gap in the market between a storefront tax preparer and a national firm with a six-figure minimum. Businesses land in it when they add a second entity, cross a state line, take on a partner, or start doing something their accounting system was never configured to handle.

That gap is what this practice is built for.

## Where we work

- **Entity structuring and restructuring** — choice of entity, holding company structures, when an S election helps and when it becomes a constraint
- **S corporation compensation** — reasonable compensation analysis with documentation that survives examination, and the distribution mechanics that follow
- **Multi-state exposure** — nexus review, apportionment, and the registration and filing obligations that follow remote employees and out-of-state customers
- **Multi-entity groups** — intercompany transactions, consolidating returns, and the reconciliations that keep the books defensible
- **Texas franchise tax** — the no-tax-due threshold, the reporting obligation that persists regardless, combined group reporting, and the public information report
- **Accounting method and system issues** — accrual conversions, revenue recognition timing, lease treatment, and cleaning up books that an ERP migration left in poor shape

## Background

The technical accounting work here comes from a decade in Big 4 and independent consulting environments — foreign currency remeasurement, lease and revenue standards, and ERP-era ledgers at companies considerably larger than yours. Applied to a small business, that experience mostly shows up as knowing which problems are real and which ones can be left alone.

We do not provide attest services. If your bank or investor requires a review or an audit, we will tell you plainly and help you scope it with a firm that does.
