Bookkeeping matters for exactly two reasons: you need to know how the business is doing, and someone has to prepare a tax return from it. Most small business books fail the first test and make the second one expensive.

## Ongoing bookkeeping

Monthly close, reconciliations, and a financial statement package with a short written summary of what changed and why. Not a data dump.

## Cleanup

More common than ongoing work, and usually the reason people call. Typical situations:

- Books that were never reconciled, or were reconciled to a plug
- A chart of accounts that grew organically and no longer maps to anything
- An accounting system migration that landed balances in the wrong places
- Cash-basis records that need to be put on accrual for a lender or a buyer
- Several years of catching up before returns can be filed

Cleanup is scoped and quoted after a diagnostic review, because the honest answer to "how long will this take" is unknowable until someone has looked.
