Return preparation for individuals, partnerships, S corporations, and estates and trusts. Federal and multi-state.

The work includes identifying every deduction and credit you are entitled to, but the more useful part is usually the review that happens first: reading what actually occurred during the year, checking it against how the third-party forms reported it, and finding the places where those two things disagree. Broker basis reporting, K-1 allocations, and equity compensation are where they disagree most often.

## Included in every engagement

- Federal and state returns, including multi-state allocation where it applies
- A review of the prior year's return — amended returns are worth filing more often than people expect
- Estimated payment calculations and a schedule for the coming year
- A written summary of what drove the result, in plain language
- Year-round access for questions, not just during filing season

## Working together

Documents are exchanged through an encrypted portal, not email attachments. We work with clients across the country; nothing about this engagement requires you to be in Houston, and most of it happens by video and phone.
